# 🚀 Skillbridge AI

Skillbridge AI is an intelligent, full-stack career and campus enablement platform designed to bridge the gap between academic learning and professional readiness. By integrating advanced automation, personalized guidance, and comprehensive workflow tools, the platform equips students with the exact skills, confidence, and visibility needed to secure top-tier job interviews.

---

## ✨ Core Features & How They Help

### 1. AI-Powered Resume & Portfolio Builder
* **What it does:** Automatically structures and optimizes resumes based on real-time industry standards and job descriptions.
* **How it helps:** Ensures your profile passes Automated Tracking Systems (ATS) and highlights your strongest technical and organizational achievements, making you instantly attractive to recruiters.

### 2. Intelligent Skill & Practice Tracker
* **What it does:** Seamlessly tracks daily problem-solving progress, technical skill development, and project milestones.
* **How it helps:** Keeps your technical edge sharp and provides verifiable proof of consistent practice—qualities that hiring managers actively look for during technical screenings.

### 3. Automated Administrative & Campus Workflows
* **What it does:** Simplifies campus services, placement cell communications, event participation, and academic assistance into a single unified dashboard.
* **How it helps:** Eliminates administrative friction, allowing you to focus your time and energy entirely on interview preparation and skill-building rather than logistics.

### 4. Direct Institutional Collaboration & Event Management
* **What it does:** Streamlines hackathons, internal institutional events, and placement drives.
* **How it helps:** Gives you direct exposure to high-impact campus events and networking opportunities that frequently lead to direct corporate referrals and recruiter connections.

---

## 🎯 How Skillbridge AI Helps You Get an Interview

* **Bypasses ATS Filters:** The platform's optimization tools ensure your resume features the right keywords, formatting, and structural hierarchy to clear initial automated screenings.
* **Showcases Consistent Growth:** By logging practical projects, continuous problem-solving metrics, and leadership experiences, it builds a compelling portfolio that gives recruiters undeniable proof of your capabilities.
* **Accelerates Placement Readiness:** By centralizing placement schedules, communication channels, and preparation resources, you never miss a crucial recruitment window, career fair, or interview deadline.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** TypeScript, React, TanStack Start
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS, Modern UI Components
* **Backend & Database:** Supabase, PLpgSQL
* **Tooling & Build:** Bun, Vite, Wrangler

---

## 📁 Project Structure

- `src/` - Core application source code, components, and views
- `supabase/` - Database schemas, migrations, and backend functions
- `.lovable/` - Development and configuration settings
